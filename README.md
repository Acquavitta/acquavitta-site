# AcquaVitta v5 — English/Portuguese + Contact Form

English is the default language. Use EN → PT in the header to switch languages.

The contact form opens in a modal and includes:
- Name
- Email
- Phone (optional)
- Product (optional)
- Message

Submissions are configured through FormSubmit to sales@acquavitta.com.

IMPORTANT: FormSubmit normally sends a one-time activation email to sales@acquavitta.com. Confirm that email before the form can forward submissions.

Upload index.html, assets/, and keep CNAME = acquavitta.com.
