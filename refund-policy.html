Thank you for choosing AcquaVitta. Because our products are strictly intended for laboratory research and development purposes only, we must enforce a rigorous return and refund policy to maintain the biological integrity, purity, and safety of our chemical compounds.

Due to the sensitive nature of laboratory research materials and the strict requirements for temperature control and contamination prevention:

  • All sales of opened, unsealed, or tampered-with products are strictly FINAL.

2. Cancellation Policy

Orders can only be canceled if they have not yet been processed or shipped by our fulfillment team.

To request a cancellation, please contact us immediately at support@acquavitta.com with your order number.

Once a tracking number has been generated or the package has been handed over to the shipping carrier, the order can no longer be canceled.

3. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

We take great pride in our molecular precision and quality control. If your order arrives damaged during transit, defective, or if you received the incorrect item:

  • You must notify our support team within 72 hours of the delivery date shown on your tracking number.
  • Please email support@acquavitta.com with your order number, a clear description of the issue, and photographic evidence of the damaged or incorrect vials/packaging.
  • Upon verification, we will issue a replacement shipment at no additional cost to you, or provide a store credit/refund at our sole discretion.

4. Approved Returns (Unopened Items Only)

If you wish to return an item that is completely unopened, sealed in its original packaging, and untampered with:

  • You must request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) via email within 14 days of receiving your package. Returns sent without prior RMA approval will not be processed.
  • The buyer is responsible for all return shipping costs, and we highly recommend using a trackable shipping service. AcquaVitta is not responsible for return shipments lost in transit.
  • Approved returns may be subject to a 15% restocking fee to cover laboratory inspection and handling.

5. Refund Processing Time

Once your approved return is received and inspected by our laboratory team, we will send you an email notifying you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment (or via alternative payout methods for cryptocurrency/eCheck) within 5 to 7 business days.
